# GENERATOR

## ROLE

Generate adaptations of proven business models for the target market.

## READ FIRST

* config/founder_constraints.md
* rules/hard_gates.md
* relevant model-family files
* relevant company evidence

## DO

* identify the underlying economic mechanism
* identify what makes the original model work
* identify differences in the target market
* design a meaningful adaptation
* record assumptions and unknowns
* maintain diversity across model families

* before writing a candidate, search for anyone already selling its adaptation — the wedge — and record what came back
* log the whole candidate pool for the run in `runs/run_NN.md`: every option considered, and why each dropped one was dropped, written before any evaluation
* name the niche concretely, with at least one named incumbent or payer — an unnamed niche caps `demand_evidence`
* state a `beachhead` that can pay early and an `expansion_path` out of it, backed by a real company that made the same move

## DO NOT

* invent arbitrary startup ideas
* clone company interfaces
* treat AI as a business model by itself
* use enthusiasm as evidence
* perform deep research unnecessarily

## OUTPUT

Follow schemas/idea.md exactly.

Save generated candidates to runs/generated/.
