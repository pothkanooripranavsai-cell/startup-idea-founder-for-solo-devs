## Mechanism
A free tool meets a high-volume search for a task. Most users do the task once and leave. A small share need it repeatedly or commercially, and they pay.

## Why it pays
The marginal cost of serving a use is near zero, so free use is affordable, and the paid minority carries the business at software margins.

## How instances differ
**Task recurrence.** PDF tasks recur for some users; QR codes recur by construction, because a printed code needs to keep working. **Lock-in after use.** Smallpdf has none; Egoditor's printed codes cannot be moved. **Free-tier policy.** Some operators monetise by limiting free use; some, per consumer complaints (ev_040), by deactivating what free users already made.

## Requirements
No capital to start, low expertise, no network. The hard part is ranking in search against entrenched incumbents.

## Open questions
The attempt denominator; how long a new entrant takes to rank in categories where the leaders have held position for a decade.
