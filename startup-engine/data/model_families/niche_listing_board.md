# Niche listing board with supply-side per-unit payment

## Mechanism

One side has a recurring need to be seen by a specific, hard-to-reach group. The operator concentrates that group's attention in one place, then sells access to it per unit.

The audience pays nothing and is the product. The supply side pays a flat fee per listing.

## Why it pays

The fee is justified by narrowness, not volume. A general board competes with every other general board on price; a board that reaches precisely the people a buyer cannot otherwise find can charge several times more per listing (`ev_007`).

The cost structure is the striking part. One instance reports roughly 99 percent margin, implying no per-unit cost at all beyond hosting and tooling (`ev_009`). The operator sells attention they do not have to buy.

Critically for a zero-capital operator, payment is **per listing**, not per subscription. Revenue does not wait for scale — the first buyer pays a full unit price.

## How instances differ

**Operator scale and audience origin** is the axis that matters most here, and the two instances sit at opposite ends.

RemoteOK is solo and unfunded at roughly 35000 dollars monthly, but it was built years ago and now carries a large accumulated audience (`ev_008`). It proves the mechanism can be *run* by one person indefinitely.

MoAIJobs was founded at the end of 2023 by a single founder, charges 199 dollars per listing, and passed 2300 dollars monthly (`ev_009`). It proves the mechanism can be *started* from nothing recently — a much more relevant demonstration, at a much more modest number.

**Seeding method** is the unrecorded axis and the one a candidate must answer. Neither record establishes how the initial audience was assembled before anyone would pay to reach it.

**Price per listing** ranges from around 99 dollars for broad boards to 600 for genuinely hard-to-reach audiences.

## Requirements

Capital: effectively none. This is the mechanism's defining advantage.

Expertise and network: both `UNKNOWN`, and the second is the live risk. The mechanism inverts the usual cold-start problem rather than removing it — instead of needing suppliers, it needs an audience before the supply side will pay.

## Open questions

How the first audience is assembled at zero cost and zero standing. Every instance on file had an audience by the time it was earning, and none of the records explain how it got there.

Whether the niche must be one the operator already belongs to. If so, this mechanism carries a hidden network requirement and Gate 3 bites.

Whether listing supply can be seeded by aggregating publicly posted listings before any buyer pays, and what that means for the value of a paid placement.
