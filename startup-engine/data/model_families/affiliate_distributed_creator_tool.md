## Mechanism
Creators pay monthly for a tool that removes hours of manual editing. Affiliates, paid only from the revenue they bring, carry distribution, so acquisition costs nothing up front.

## Why it pays
The job recurs every time a creator publishes, and the affiliate share is paid after the customer pays. The weakness is churn: the one instance reports about 15 percent a month (ev_067).

## How instances differ
See the differentiating axes. OpusClip runs the same job venture-funded (ev_073).

## Requirements
No capital or network to start if the free tier carries no compute cost.
