## Mechanism
The platform aggregates the buyers and the intent. Merchants search its app store for a job, install, and pay through the platform's billing, so the developer needs no audience and no sales process.

## Why it pays
Buyers are pre-qualified, billing is built in, and Shopify keeps nothing on a developer's first million dollars of lifetime revenue (ev_030).

## How instances differ
**Category contestedness.** Judge.me entered a crowded category and won on price and support; CedCommerce built many narrow listings. **Platform dependency.** Every instance lives on terms the platform can change.

## Requirements
No capital or network. Real exposure to platform decisions: the platform can build the same function natively, as it did for Meta and Google tracking (ev_036).

## Open questions
The revenue distribution behind the review concentration; how many apps were delisted.
