## Mechanism
Track public numbers about the participants on a platform, publish them free to draw search traffic, charge for history, alerts and depth, and expand one platform at a time.

## Why it pays
History is the moat: a newcomer cannot backfill years of daily counts. Both instances began on one platform or one game and expanded - Social Blade across social platforms, Wargraphs from a website to an in-game app. Both depend on access terms the tracked platforms can change.
