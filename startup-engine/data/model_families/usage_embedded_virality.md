## Mechanism
Each use produces something seen by non-users: a form someone fills in, an email someone receives. The output carries the product's name, and some recipients become users.

## Why it pays
Acquisition cost falls toward zero as usage grows, and a small paying share (about 2 percent at Tally, ev_043) carries the business.

## How instances differ
**Exposures per use.** A form reaches its respondents once; an email signature reaches every recipient of every email. **Recipient fit.** The loop only works where the people seeing the badge could plausibly want the product. Hotmail's footer (ev_044) is the classic proof in email, but it was venture-funded from the start and is recorded as mechanism proof, not as a zero-capital instance.

## Requirements
A free tier generous enough that people actually use it, and outputs that reach non-users.

## Open questions
The loop coefficient for any category other than forms; whether recipients in business email convert at useful rates.
