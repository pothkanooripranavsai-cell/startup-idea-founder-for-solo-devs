# MoAIJobs

## What it does

A jobs board for one narrow vertical, run by a single founder since the end of 2023.

## How it makes money

Employers pay a flat 199 dollars per vacancy. Candidates pay nothing.

Payment is per unit, so the first employer produces real revenue without any subscription threshold or traffic minimum being crossed.

## What made it work

The reported 99 percent margin is the salient fact. It implies essentially no cost of goods — no data purchased, no per-customer expense, nothing that scales with volume. The operator sells concentrated attention they did not have to buy.

Founder advantage is `UNKNOWN`, and that gap matters more here than the revenue figure. A board needs an audience before an employer will pay to reach it, and nothing sourced explains how this founder assembled one.

## How it differs from other instances

Against RemoteOK: far smaller, but far more relevant. RemoteOK demonstrates a mechanism being run by one person at scale after years of audience accumulation. MoAIJobs demonstrates one being started recently from nothing, which is the question a founder at zero standing actually needs answered.

Against the comparison-platform instances: the payment is per listing rather than per click or per lead, so revenue does not wait on traffic volume.

## Open questions

How the candidate audience was assembled before employers would pay. Whether the founder belonged to the niche already. Whether 2300 dollars monthly is a plateau or a waypoint.
