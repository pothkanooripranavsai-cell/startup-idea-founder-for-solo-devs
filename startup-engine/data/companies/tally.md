## What it does
A form builder with a generous free plan.

## How it makes money
Pro subscriptions from a small minority of users; the free plan is the acquisition channel.

## What made it work
Free forms carry a Made with Tally badge, so every respondent is shown the product (ev_043). The founders credit word of mouth, organic search and, in 2025, AI assistants' recommendations.

## How it differs from other instances
Each use of the product is seen by people who are not users, so distribution grows with usage rather than with founder effort.

## Open questions
What share of signups came from the badge specifically; the founders' starting position.
