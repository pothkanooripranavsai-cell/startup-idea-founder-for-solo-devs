## What it does
Lets anyone create QR codes, including dynamic codes that point through its own redirect so the destination can be changed after printing, with scan analytics.

## How it makes money
Subscriptions for dynamic codes, analytics and team features. The trigger for payment is continuing to need a printed code to work or to change where it points.

## What made it work
An early position in a category that later became ubiquitous, and a product whose output, once printed, cannot be moved. Consumer complaints describe the same lock-in being used to force payment after trials (ev_040).

## How it differs from other instances
Unlike Smallpdf, each unit of work leaves a permanent physical artefact that depends on the operator's servers, which turns a one-off task into a recurring relationship.
