# Techjockey

## What it does

An Indian B2B software discovery and purchase platform. Buyers compare products, read ratings, book demos, take free consultation from advisors, and can purchase through the platform.

## How it makes money

The supply and transaction side pays. Buyers are explicitly not charged — comparison, consultation and demos are free to them.

## What made it work

The family record's premise holds here: buyers seek help with a decision that matters, and suppliers pay for the resulting intent. Techjockey added human advisory on top of the catalogue and still gave it away free, which suggests the supply-side economics comfortably cover advisory delivery.

Founder starting advantage is `UNKNOWN`.

## How it differs from other instances

Against NerdWallet, G2 and Idealo: Techjockey operates in the target market, and it is the only instance on file with `revenue_evidenced: true`.

It also blends all three information sources — vendor listings like Idealo, user reviews like G2, and advisory resembling editorial judgement — rather than specialising in one, and it closes the transaction on-platform rather than referring out.
