## What it does
Browser-based tools for converting, compressing and editing PDFs.

## How it makes money
A free tier with usage limits, and a paid Pro subscription for people who hit them repeatedly. Advertising paid the early costs.

## What made it work
Answering a task people search for constantly, and charging only the minority whose use is frequent.

## How it differs from other instances
The task is one-off for most users, so revenue depends on a small share of heavy users rather than on any lock-in.

## Open questions
Founder starting position; the conversion rate from free to paid; how much traffic still comes from search.
