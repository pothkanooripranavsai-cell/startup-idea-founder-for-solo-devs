# NerdWallet

## What it does

Publishes editorial comparisons and reviews of consumer financial products, and routes readers to providers at the point of decision.

## How it makes money

Financial institutions pay when a reader converts — affiliate commissions and per-application or per-account lead fees. The reader pays nothing.

This makes revenue concentrated among a relatively small number of large payers, and sensitive to those payers' own marketing budgets and credit appetite.

## What made it work

The mechanism rests on sustained editorial output ranking in organic search for high-intent queries. That is a publishing operation with a long payback period before the corpus ranks, not a software operation.

Whether the founders began with financial-sector credibility is `UNKNOWN` and matters: in a regulated category, trust is part of what converts.

## How it differs from other instances

Against G2: NerdWallet produces its own information rather than collecting it from users. It has no two-sided community to seed, but also no user-generated content to compound — it must keep paying writers.

Against Idealo: NerdWallet's information is written judgment, not ingested data. It has no merchant integration requirement, but its corpus cannot be refreshed automatically.

Its regulatory exposure is materially higher than either.
