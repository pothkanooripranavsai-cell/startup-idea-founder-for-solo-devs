## What it does
AI captions, clip extraction and short-form editing for creators.

## How it makes money
Subscriptions billed monthly or annually, paid in advance.

## What made it work
Solving the founder's own editing problem in a market where creators already spent hours by hand, then distribution through influencers and an affiliate programme paid only from revenue.

## How it differs from other instances
Bootstrapped where its largest competitor, OpusClip, raised about 50 million USD (ev_073).

## Open questions
The founder's starting audience; whether early influencer deals were paid before revenue; how the business carries 15 percent monthly churn.
