## What it does
A product-reviews app for Shopify merchants.

## How it makes money
Monthly subscriptions billed through Shopify, priced far below the incumbents it displaced.

## What made it work
Entering a category held by expensive incumbents with a much lower price and heavy support, inside a marketplace where merchants were already searching for the job.

## How it differs from other instances
It entered a contested category and won on price and support, rather than finding an empty one.
