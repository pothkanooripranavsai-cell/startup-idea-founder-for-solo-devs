# RankInPublic

## What it does

Runs public A-versus-B voting matchups between submitted products, explicitly modelled on LMArena.ai's comparison format, applied to SaaS products instead of language models.

## How it makes money

Submission is free. Paid directory listings and premium placement are sold to the founders whose products are being compared.

## What made it work

Not the comparison mechanic alone — every review site has that. The growth event coincided with making each matchup page something its participants had a personal reason to share: a founder whose product is winning, or simply competing publicly, will post that page to their own audience because it reflects on them.

That converts every participant into a distribution channel the operator never had to build or buy access to. The founder's own account attributes the revenue jump specifically to this mechanic being added, not to increased outreach or spend.

## Why it matters to this project

It is the first solo, named, revenue-disclosed instance this project has found of `artifact_embedded_distribution` — the same mechanism `ev_025` documents at 100-million-dollar-ARR scale for G2's badge widgets. G2 was never solo; RankInPublic was never large. Together they are the pairing this project has needed since run 01: a mechanism proven at real scale, shown operable from zero.

## Open questions

Whether the revenue jump is durable or a viral spike that will regress. Retention and repeat-purchase rate are not sourced. Whether the mechanic generalises beyond a novelty comparison format to a category where participants have less ego investment in the outcome is untested.
