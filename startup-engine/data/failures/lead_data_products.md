# Curated lead and contact database products

## What they built

Searchable databases of business contacts and company records, sold by subscription or per record to sales and marketing teams.

## What actually goes wrong

Two things at once, and either alone would be disqualifying here.

The data is bought, not produced. Per-record pricing means inventory cost precedes revenue, which is precisely the ordering the founder's constraints exist to exclude.

The category is held by funded platforms, and the entry route offered to newcomers is white-label reselling of the same underlying data — a position with no differentiation from the supplier and no leverage over it.

## Does it still apply

Yes. Neither cause is a timing accident, so neither is waiting to be dissolved by a new capability.

## Open questions

The sources examined were vendor and reseller marketing pages, which is promotional tier and self-interested in presenting the reselling opportunity favourably. The conclusion drawn here runs against their pitch rather than with it, which is some protection, but this record is an INFERENCE and not a sourced post-mortem of a specific failure.

Recorded so this path is not re-proposed without someone first answering the per-record cost problem.
