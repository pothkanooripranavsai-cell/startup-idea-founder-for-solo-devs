The second record in the corpus whose publisher has no stake in the claim being true, after `ev_015`.

Gaps publishes revenue reports across industries and does not sell job board software. Every other source on this mechanism gathered across four runs is published by someone selling the tooling to build one, which makes their revenue figures marketing for the tool. This one is not.

## Why it matters more than the count suggests

Runs 02 and 03 carried this mechanism on two solo instances. Six changes what can honestly be claimed about `solo_transfer`. The rubric reserves a 5 for mechanisms "already demonstrably run by solo operators at zero capital," and two instances did not support that. Six named boards, solo-run, with disclosed revenue, does.

The same table lists funded operators at far larger numbers - ZipRecruiter at 474M annually against 769M raised, Zen Educate at 22M against 71M raised. The mechanism therefore scales with capital but does not require it, which is exactly the distinction `solo_transfer` is built to detect.

## The monetisation split

The six solo instances do not all charge the same side.

RemoteOK and MoAIJobs charge the supply side per listing, at 299 dollars and 199 dollars respectively. Remote Rocketship charges the demand side by subscription, at 5 dollars weekly or 18 monthly. OnlineJobs.ph charges employers for subscription access to candidates rather than per posting.

Both payer directions are independently evidenced as solo-viable. Run 02 treated the demand-side variant as an untested inversion; it is not untested.

## Not relied upon

The funded operators' revenue figures are recorded but carry no weight on any gate. They describe what the mechanism does with capital behind it, which is not the question being asked.
