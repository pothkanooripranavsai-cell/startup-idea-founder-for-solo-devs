Review aggregators and an independent blog are community sources, so the pattern is held as INFERENCE; the FTC guide is primary but states only a disclosure rule, not action against any named company. Claims elsewhere that the FTC has scrutinised these sites come from competitor blogs and are not used. The pattern matters twice: it shows printed codes create switching costs customers pay to avoid, and it shows the market leader is distrusted.
