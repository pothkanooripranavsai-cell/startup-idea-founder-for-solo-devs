The most consequential finding of this research pass, and it is unfavourable.

Tier and quality caveats, stated plainly: this is a company blog serving independent consultants, so promotional tier. The ninety percent figure carries no stated methodology or sample. Under `rules/evidence_policy.md` a promotional source is never sole support for a viability claim, and that discipline applies symmetrically — it is not sole grounds to fail a gate either. The geography is US, not the target market, so it does not establish local conditions.

What it does establish, corroborated in substance by the other sources in this pass listing network and referral as the primary channels: the dominant first-client route for solo advisory work is a pre-existing network. This founder has none.

The alternative channels those same sources document — cold outreach, community participation, teaching a free class or workshop, guest content, partnering with a non-competing independent to borrow their audience — are real and unpaid. They are described as slow and secondary, and they are the reason Gate 7 passes while the distribution dimension scores badly.
