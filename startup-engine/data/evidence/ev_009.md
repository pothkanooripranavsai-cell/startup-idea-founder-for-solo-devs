More useful than the RemoteOK figure for this founder, because it is recent and started from nothing. Founded at the end of 2023, solo, reaching a few thousand dollars monthly.

The 99 percent margin claim is the material one under a zero-capital constraint. It implies a cost base of hosting and tooling with no per-customer cost that scales, which is what Gate 1 requires.

Revenue at this level is modest and self-reported through a roundup. It establishes that the mechanism produces real money at solo scale from a standing start, not that it produces a living.
