Classified `mixed` rather than `supports` deliberately, because the honest reading of this record cuts both ways and a later pass should not be able to quote only the favourable half.

## The favourable half

The first audience cost nothing and required no standing. It was a post to a public community by someone with no following, no reputation in the space, and no budget. Gate 7 asks whether a plausible unpaid first-customer path exists that is accessible to a founder with no audience. Here is one that was actually used.

## The unfavourable half

One viral post is an outcome, not a method. A hundred and fifty thousand visitors in a day from a single community post is a tail event. Treating it as the acquisition plan would be planning to win a lottery.

The correct reading is narrow: it establishes that free community posting is *permitted and non-trivial* as a channel, not that it reliably produces an audience. Any candidate resting its distribution score on reproducing this is scoring a lottery ticket.

## What is actually durable

The sustained traffic is attributed to search, not to the viral event. That is the reproducible channel, and it is a direct consequence of the mechanism rather than of luck: aggregating thousands of structured public listings produces thousands of indexable pages targeting long-tail queries that buyers are actively typing.

The distribution advantage is therefore built into what the operator assembles, rather than bolted on afterwards. That is durable and it is the part a candidate may lean on.
