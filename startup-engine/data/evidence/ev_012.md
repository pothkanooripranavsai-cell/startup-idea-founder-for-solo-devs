The single most useful piece of evidence gathered across all three runs, because it addresses the constraint that has blocked every candidate so far: finding buyers who have money and a problem, without an audience, a reputation, or an advertising budget.

Both sources sell signal-based selling tools and publish this as content marketing, so they are promotional tier and share an underlying source type. Under `rules/evidence_policy.md` promotional sources cannot be sole support for a viability claim.

What makes this claim unusually safe despite the tier is that it is structural rather than statistical. That a posting implies approved budget follows from how organisations authorise headcount, and the vendors have no particular incentive to misstate a mechanism that is independently checkable. It is recorded as FACT for the structural claim only.

The practical consequence: an open role is a public, timestamped, budget-approved declaration of an unmet need, published free, continuously refreshed, and readable by anyone.
