Both organisations sell job board software and publish this as content marketing, so they share an underlying source type and the corroboration is weaker than two independent sources would be.

Promotional tier. Under `rules/evidence_policy.md` this cannot be sole support for a viability claim, and the revenue projections in particular are vendor-published and should be read as marketing rather than measurement.

What it does establish is the structural point that matters: pricing is per posting and paid by the employer. The supply side pays, the job seeker does not, and payment is per unit rather than requiring subscription scale.
