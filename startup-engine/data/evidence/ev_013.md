Deliberately classified INFERENCE rather than FACT, and the distinction matters.

The directional claim is credible and is what any candidate should lean on: contacting someone in the window where the problem is active and the budget unspent beats contacting them at random.

The specific figures should not be quoted as measurements. They share an underlying source type with `ev_012`, all come from vendors selling signal tooling, and none carries a methodology. Three separate outlets repeating the same vendor-origin numbers is one source, not three.

Also recorded: the timing window is narrow. The sources agree a posting is worth far more in its first days than three weeks later, which constrains how a candidate built on this signal must operate.
