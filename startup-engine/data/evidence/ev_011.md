Deliberately classified HYPOTHESIS rather than FACT.

This is a single community-tier post describing an unnamed founder, with no corroboration and no verifiable identity behind the numbers. The figures are precise and attractive, which is exactly the shape of a claim that should be treated with suspicion rather than adopted because it is convenient.

It may well be true. It is not evidence. It is recorded so the numbers are not quietly promoted to fact later, and so a future pass knows this claim was seen and deliberately not relied upon.
