The solo-scale proof for a growth mechanism this project has not previously used: the artifact a participant receives, when the participant shares it, recruits the next participant from an audience the operator never had to reach directly.

## The mechanism, stated plainly

Set up a competitive comparison between things people submitted (SaaS products, in this instance), let the public vote, and give each submitter a public page showing their result. Submitters share that page to their own following because it is about them and reflects on them — not because the operator asked them to promote the platform.

Distribution is produced by *participation itself*, not by an audience the operator built beforehand or a corpus the operator assembled from public data. This is a third distinct distribution shape alongside `niche_listing_board`'s concentrated-audience model and `public_corpus_aggregation`'s search-surface model.

## Why community tier, and what that limits

This is a single founder's own account, unverified by any independent source, and the founder is the only party who benefits from the number sounding impressive. It is retained as FACT rather than downgraded to HYPOTHESIS because it specifies a concrete before-and-after figure with a stated cause, rather than a vague success claim, and because the underlying mechanism it describes is independently corroborated at much larger scale in `ev_025`.

It cannot solely support a scoring claim above the community-tier ceiling on its own merits. Its role here is to establish that the mechanism is solo-executable at all, which `ev_025` cannot do by itself since G2 was never solo.

## What is not established

Retention, unit economics beyond the revenue figure, and whether the jump is durable rather than a temporary spike coincident with a viral moment. The record captures a growth event, not a steady-state business.
