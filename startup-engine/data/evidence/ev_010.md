Community tier, which under `rules/evidence_policy.md` cannot alone support a FACT about viability. It is recorded as FACT for a narrower claim than it may appear to support: that this practice is documented and described, not that it reliably works.

What makes it matter is structural rather than empirical. This is the only mechanism encountered so far whose defining property is that revenue arrives before the build. That is the exact ordering Gate 8 requires, and it is rare.
