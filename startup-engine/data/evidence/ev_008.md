The strongest single piece of evidence gathered for the listing-board mechanism, because it speaks to the constraint that usually kills a candidate: whether the mechanism survives without a team or capital. Here it demonstrably does.

The underlying source is the operator's own public revenue reporting, relayed by a third party. Self-reported revenue is not audited and the operator has an interest in the figure. Recorded as FACT that this is the reported figure, not as an audited one.

Note what it does not establish. This operator built the board years ago and now has a large audience. It shows the mechanism can be run solo. It does not show it can be started from zero standing today.
