Establishes that the substitution is a real and recognised purchasing pattern: an organisation with an unfilled role does sometimes buy the outcome as a service instead of hiring for it.

Promotional tier — both sources sell fractional or recruiting services and benefit from the pattern being seen as normal. That self-interest is worth holding in mind, though the existence of an established market for fractional work is independently observable.

Two limits this record does not overcome. It says nothing about whether such buyers will engage an unknown solo provider rather than an established firm, which is the same standing problem recorded in `ev_004` and `ev_006`. And it describes senior fractional roles, which is a different purchase from operational coverage.
