The single best-matched source company found across six runs of this project, against the founder's stated source band: a real company with a real team, which began at genuinely zero capital and with zero audience.

## Why this record matters more than its individual facts

Every prior run faced the same trade-off. Category leaders like G2 or NerdWallet prove a mechanism at scale but carry capital, team and network that cannot be stripped away, so `solo_transfer` can never honestly score high. Solo operators at a few thousand dollars a month strip away cleanly but prove nothing about a ceiling, and the founder has since excluded them as source material outright.

Levels.fyi is the first source that is neither. Two engineers, a Google Form, a spreadsheet, and a static page on S3 — and the same entity now employs roughly 48 people and serves about 3 million people a month. The start is reproducible under this founder's constraints; the ceiling is real.

## The three properties being cited

**Zero capital is literal, not approximate.** Google Forms, Google Sheets, and S3 static hosting, at a stated cost below a cup of coffee per year. This is not a candidate rounding infrastructure down to zero — it is a real company's actual reported starting cost.

**The cold start required no audience.** The founders seeded traffic by answering questions people were already asking, on forums where they were already asking them, with a link to the answer. No following, no reputation, no advertising. `ev_018` recorded a comparable free-community start for a different mechanism, but that one depended on a single viral post and was recorded with that caution; this one is a repeatable act rather than a lottery outcome.

**The dataset compounds into the moat.** 245,000 data points across 5,000 companies is not something a competitor can buy or replicate quickly, and it grew from three companies' leveling information.

## Caution

The scale figures are recent and the origin details are reported second-hand through interviews and profiles rather than from a primary filing. The founders' own detailed account sits behind a paywall and was not read for this record.

The company has since taken investment from two institutional investors, so it is not bootstrapped end to end. That does not affect the claim being cited — which concerns how it started and what it grew into — but a candidate must not cite this record as evidence that the mechanism scales to 48 employees without capital, because that is not what it shows.
